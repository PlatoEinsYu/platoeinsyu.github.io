### Standard Seminars

- Sheaves on manifolds.
- Unipotent ideal and unipotent representations.
- Theta correspondence, over non-archimedean field.
- The Langlands Classification and Irreducible Characters for Real Reductive Groups (ABV).
- Relative Langlands (BZSV).

### Snack Seminars

- Coexeter groups and Hecke algebra.
- BGG-Category.
- p-adic Lie groups.

- Elliptic curves.
- Admissible representations of p-adic reductive groups.
- Symplectic local root numbers, central critical L-values, restriction problems in the representation theory of classical groups (GGP).
